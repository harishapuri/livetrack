A system on one computer keeps an ordered step cursor aligned with a live electronic form. Queue cards, each an ordered list of field steps, have a run state of idle, running, or paused. A browser extension receives the current step over a loopback bridge on that computer and applies it only while the form tab is visible. A matcher binds a live control to a step and withholds completion of a later step that matches only a short option label or a generic selector. A placeholder or unselected prompt counts as no value. Pause stops further writes and leaves the form with the operator. Resume continues at the first incomplete step. One failed step is repaired from a capture of that step. Unmatched work stays a draft until approval creates a card. Extra answers are stored apart from the authored steps.
